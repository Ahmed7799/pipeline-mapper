Village Pipeline Mapper

Map village water pipelines on satellite imagery, number the nodes automatically and print a clean drawing.

Sign in to continue

How it works
  1. Draw. Trace each pipe on the map. Pipes split at nodes and crossings, and nodes are numbered for you.
  2. Describe. Choose distribution or pumping main, existing or required, material, diameter and depth. Add landmarks such as OHSR and DPS.
  3. Share. Print the drawing with a summary table, or export KML, CSV and a backup. Your work is saved on this phone and works offline.

After the first sign-in the app keeps working offline on this phone.
